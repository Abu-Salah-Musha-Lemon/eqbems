# Vision and Objectives

## Vision

Provide a dependable platform for institutions to manage reusable question content and prepare controlled, traceable examinations.

## Mission

Reduce fragmented manual administration while maintaining clear accountability, confidentiality, and historical integrity for question and exam content.

## Objectives

- Create a single, structured source for approved questions.
- Reduce duplication with taxonomy, search, and reuse rules.
- Make question approval and exam preparation traceable.
- Keep institution and user data scoped to legitimate access.
- Preserve exact exam content after approval.
- Make grading/result workflows auditable when delivered.
- Maintain a reproducible codebase with tests, migrations, and documentation.

## Guardrails

- Security and correctness take precedence over convenience for exam content.
- Features are not considered complete until acceptance criteria and tests pass.
- Architecture should remain a modular monolith until measurable scaling/team constraints justify another approach.
- Do not claim a specific performance, availability, or compliance level without measurement/review.
